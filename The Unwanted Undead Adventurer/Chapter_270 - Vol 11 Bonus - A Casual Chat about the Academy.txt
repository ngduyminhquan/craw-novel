Vol 11 Bonus - A Casual Chat about the Academy

“Come to think of it...” Lorraine said, as though an idea had just struck her. We were in the middle of having dinner.

“Something on your mind?” I asked.

“It’s just, you know how I attended the Empire’s Academy for a while?”

“Yeah.”

“Did you ever think of attending one, Rentt?”

“Wha...?”

The idea had never even occurred to me before. Being a kid from the sticks, places like the Academies had always seemed like they were in an entirely different world to mine. But now that I was giving it some thought...

“I guess if the chance had presented itself, I might have gone,” I said.

“Surrounded by peers my age, all of us pouring our best into our studies? That kind of friendly competition sounds nice.”

When I’d learned from the hunter and herbalist in my home village, it had mostly just been me, or occasionally a handful of other kids. However, children attended the schools in big cities in huge numbers. The rivalries that sprang up in places like those sounded kind of fun.

Lorraine, however, made an indecipherable expression. “That’s...certainly idealistic,” she said.

I inclined my head to the side. “Is the reality different?”

“Pretty much. In my case, they all tried to sabotage me. Since the Empire lets gifted individuals skip grades one after the other, the other students must have felt uncomfortable to have a girl seven or eight years their junior sitting in the same classroom as them. One thing they did was hide my textbooks, for example.”

“What are they, kids? Er...I guess they would have been, actually.”

“Mmm. Sixteen- or seventeen-year-old kids, granted.”

“Yeah, no justifying that then. They must’ve known what they were doing, at that age.”

“At the Academies, they’re treated as children, though. It’s why they get up to pointless mischief.”

“Did you never fight back?”

“Don’t be silly. I laid all kinds of traps for them. Some even traumatized their victims, but, well, you reap what you sow.”

“Let me correct what I said earlier. You acted like a kid too, Lorraine.”

“Of course. I was around ten at the time.”

“Now you’re just playing dirty...”

Lorraine laughed. “Anyway, my point is that the Academies aren’t necessarily sunshine and daisies. Besides, from a learning standpoint, you’re already a match for an Academy graduate, Rentt. You’ve read all the books in my home.”

“You think? I suppose there’s no need to go then.”

“Still, I think I would’ve liked to attend one with you, Rentt.”

“That does sound nice. I guess if we ever get the chance...”

“Mmm. I’ll look forward to it.”
