Why Only Me? – 7
================

[Graaaaaaaaaaaar!]

An Ice demon roared loudly. When it extended its left arm, a stream of pure energy froze everything in its path.

"Carina!"

"I know!"

When Michael Smithson shouted, courageously redirecting the arm with his shield, Carina Malatesta, who had aimed for that moment, cast restraint magic to bind the demon.

"Hhp!"

When the ice demon was firmly held in place, Takagaki Asuha suddenly appeared from behind it and tied its neck with her whip. However, that lacked the destructive ability to finish it off!

"Hhaaaaaaap!"

"Still not enough, kugh!"

"Charge, Michael!"

The battle with the demon only ended after another 30 minutes of cruel battle. If Na YuNa hadn't blessed them midway, there would've been another dangerous moment.

"... I guess we just barely won that one." Michael panted.

"Michael, good job."

"Carina, you too."

Michael Smithson, who had it the hardest since he was in charge of defence, broke off the ice off his shield and panted heavily. Carina Malatesta wiped the sweat off his forehead with a handkerchief. Ignoring their flirting (she got used to it by now), Takagaki Asuha checked her level.

"Although it's definitely insanely hard... I levelled up again. I'm already at level 250!"

"How is it still so hard even though we're levelling up so fast?"

Erta gave a simple and clear explanation as she prepared to summon the next monster.

"Because the levels of the summoned monsters increase as you three increase in level."

"You devil!"

"A devil? That's quite nice to hear after having been called an 'angel' to death!"

Erta's refreshing smile was angelic!

"Perhaps anyone who gets involved with Mister Unique transforms into terrifying beings...."

"I'm afraid we might become like that someday."

Erta snorted after hearing their words.

"You three are still far from catching up to him."

"Well, that's of course...."

Michael Smithson subconsciously nodded his head. His gaze headed there. A gigantic dragon and a single human were fighting.

[I'll kill youuuuuuuuuuu!]

"How do you intend to do that? When will you ever catch me? I think it'll take a shorter amount of time for me to die of old age and be laid in my grave than being caught by something like you!"

[Kraaaaaaah!]

"Roar louder! You can't even scare an earthworm with that!"

[Yu IlHaaaaaaaaaaaan!]

"Your steps are so weak and wobbly that someone might see and think you're an infant! Should I sing a lullaby?"

Watching Yu IlHan drive the frightening dragon into a corner, even while not forgetting to inflict copious mental damage, they felt like they were worse than ants. And the one who revived that frightening dragon was Yu IlHan himself!

Carina Malatesta asked with an absurd face.

"How long has it been since Mr. Yu IlHan started fighting that dragon?"

"Let's see. There's around a month until the end of the duration of the barrier, so I guess they've been doing that for an entire year and three months."

"Without sleeping even once?"

"Other than when bathing in the Cradle of Miracles. He indeed never rested nor slept."

Michael Smithson became silent. Right now, Yu IlHan was dashing left and right through the air, clearly still full of vitality even as he swung his spear. The fear-striking flash that seemed to split the universe itself whenever he swung the spear and hit the dragon made him realize that Yu IlHan was not the slightest bit tired.

"We used the Cradle of Miracles until now too...."

"But resonance is not something that's easy. But that shouldn't be the only thing."

Although Yu IlHan was indeed exceptional, training for such a long period without sleep wasn't something that could be easily challenged just because one had the capability to do so. It was only possible because of his strong mental fortitude.

Although it was frustrating, Michael Smithson admitted for the first time that he was inferior to Yu IlHan.

"I do not think he's in the right in all things but... There's at least one thing to admire about him."

"You can also become stronger."

"No, Carina. I do not want to be greedy like that any longer. I'm sick and tired of trying to grab everything and failing to do so."

He heaved a short sigh and raised his shield again. The ragged breath had also calmed down.

"I will become a shield that protects you. I won't be greedy for everything, and only polish what I can do to the extreme."

"If that's what you wish... my love."

"Yes, yes. Have a happy love life."

This couple was so flirtatious, alright! Whatever they did eventually reached a pink-colored ending!

Takagaki Asuha, who had watched the two flirt for more than a year, looked away from them and towards Yu IlHan. He was what drove her forward. He was driving a terrifying dragon that she could never even dream of fighting to a corner! Even though he denied it, he must be the reincarnation of a god! She was happy just watching like this.

"Looks like you've all recovered."

"Ah, aah."

When she realized, Erta was staring at her lightly.

"If you wish to be of his help, you don't have time to rest so leisurely, no?"

"Of course. I've levelled up just now, so I'm fine physically. Please summon the next monster. ...if possible, above that couple's heads."

"That sounds interesting."

It wasn't just the three clan masters. Yumir, Kang MiRae, Na YuNa, Kim YeSeul, Liera, Helièna, Kang HaJin and even the Dragon's Army were doing what they could within the barrier. The year and a half that lasted for not even an instant – Earth's battle force was being upgraded at lightspeed.

Of course, the one with the most growth was of course Yu IlHan.

[Krhoaaaaaaaaaaah!]

Roared Echjar. His body no longer had a heart, nor any spines or ribs – heck, he was missing flesh and blood as well – but with Yu IlHan's divine-tier Blacksmithing and Mana crafting, he could revive it with the power to overwhelm any 6th-class beings.

[You... what is it that you want!]

"You should know already."

However, even with the overwhelming strength, Echjar could not win against Yu IlHan. Echjar had the advantage when he was first revived, but as Yu IlHan adapted to the Flamesoul dragon body throughout the fight, the situation was eventually reversed.

[I will never submit to you! It is not my loss even if I lose against you with this inferior body!]

"What are you saying? You lost to me the moment you died. This is only the process of snapping your morale."

[You defeated me with a cowardly method! Had I retained my original power, I would not lose my life to the likes of you!]

Yu IlHan snorted.

"You defeated your enemies with curses, and you call me a coward, eh? You are a wimp unsuited to be a dragon."

[Krhaaaaaaaaaaaaahk!]

"It's not the strongest that wins; it's the victor that is strong. Didn't I say so? I'll free you if you win against me. And you nodded your head at that time. And now that you lose so easily, you're coming up with excuses? Were you really born a dragon?"

[T-this coward...!]

Orochi, who had also heard those lines for like the millionth time now, muttered powerlessly.

[You're so evil. I think my morale is going to be snapped before his....]

"I need to trample on him a little more."

Yu IlHan leapt several times in the air as he said that. Wherever he went, his spear drew the air, and the Great Cosmos-severing Spear that followed it struck Echjar's injured body once again.

[Krhaaa, it's painful! It's PAINFUL!]

"You're so pathetic! Why don't you just commit suicide instead? If you have the pride of a dragon, then why don't you just do that!"

[Krhaaaaaaaaaaah!]

Once, he was called the Dragon of Despair, Echjar. He swung his tail to attack Yu IlHan, but as Yu IlHan had recorded all of its movements in the past, it only looked like he was acting cute. In fact, he even traced the tail's movements, flew towards where it would lose its force, and grabbed it when it had completely lost its power, twisting it apart.

[Kugh, Khaaaaaaaaak!]

At that moment, Echjar cried.

[I... lost.]

He admitted defeat for the first time. After such a long period of battle, his mentality had finally snapped before his body did.

[I will acknowledge you as the master of dragons.]

Unsatisfied with his tone, Yu IlHan twisted a little harder and checked again.

"Say that again."

[Khhhh! I acknowledge you as the ruler of us dragons!]

Yu IlHan let go of the tail and floated upwards. At that moment, new text engraved itself on Yu Ilhan's retina.

[Although incomplete, you have received the declaration of defeat from the thought-soul of the strongest dragon in existence, the Dragon of Despair.] The records you possess as a dragon become enhanced. The power to rule over dragons activates a little more.]

There was no way the high and mighty 7th-class dragon would admit defeat just because Yu IlHan beat him up a little. It was all possible because he had acquired the qualifications to rule the dragons by completing his armour.

In any case, Yu IlHan had achieved what he had set out to do. Now, Echjar was his.

"Good, then you're on my side as well from now on."

[You finally did it, you malicious master.]

The Rule skill at the level of mastery covered Echjar's entire body. With both his body and mind tattered, Echjar cast aside the pride he had accumulated over countless years and agreed to become Yu IlHan's subordinate.

"Echjar, let's get along well from now on."

[I acknowledge you as my master... so you must become the strongest.]

['Echjar' has become your subordinate.]

He finally succeeded in all the objectives he had set within the barrier. Yu IlHan's lips curved into a smile.

"Good, then let's switch places now."

[That body of the terrifying dragon is uncomfortable in itself but... I guess I can't help it.]

[Fuu... for me, this is more comfortable.]

There was no need to hesitate anymore. Yu IlHan immediately activated 'Creation' to switch Orochi and Echjar.

Echjar would be able to adapt more easily to the Body of the Human Dragon, which contained his very own essence, and Orochi would also find the simpleton 7th-class dragon's body a better fit than a God-ranked artifact.

"Orochi, what should we do? Leave the body as it is?"

[It is insanely uncomfortable. Make it a humanoid form instead, master.]

"Good, leave that to me. I'll compress the power without losing any of it."

Just as Yu IlHan was about to commence his last piece of work in the barrier, Mystic, who had stayed obedient until now, raised her voice in complaint.

[Master, me too! I also want a body!]

"You... can just wait a little more."

[You're too much! You idiotic master! I also want a body like Orochi! A cute little girl!]

[Haha, brat. Go break dance or something with that gigantic body of yours.]

[Orochi, you idioooooooooot!]

Mystic's young-sounding voice filled the barrier. Yu IlHan laughed and picked up his hammer.

There was still a month left until the barrier was dispelled, plenty of time to create another masterpiece.
