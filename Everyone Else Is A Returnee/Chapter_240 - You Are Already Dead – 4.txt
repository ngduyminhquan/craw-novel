You Are Already Dead – 4
========================

As time went by, the Destruction Demon Army camp, abandoned by the higher-ups and Teraka, was defeated by Yu IlHan and his army. Yu IlHan left his allies the opportunity to gain experience without taking a single kill after reaching level 294. As a result, when the remaining Destruction Demon Army was reduced by half, each group had killed a higher being at least once.

Not only did that lead to their levelling up, but it also gave them the confidence to defeat higher existences.

"We really became stronger!"

"Captain made us stronger!"

[Kuhaaaaaaang!]

[Follow our one and only lord. He shall lead us to becoming strong!]

No one on the battlefield right now was not going wild. The Dragon's Army, the wolfkin, and the elves as well! Paté especially, after becoming able to wield the undead of higher existences, received meticulous protection from his allies and killed one higher existence after another to increase the size of his undead army!

[Khaaak! To think I'd be in such a miserable state against mere lower existences!]

[These flames... I resent these flames that bind me. I curse you. Yu IlHan! You shall definitely meet your end at the hands of the Destruction Demon Army!]

The only reason the army could fight the Destruction Demon Army was Yu IlHan's Falling Down skill. At first, the Destruction Demon Army, which had given up killing Yu IlHan and fought against the lower existences thinking that they might really be put in a serious state at this rate, decided to kill Yu IlHan by combining their powers, but unfortunately, Yu IlHan was the strongest one here.

"Uooooooooh!"

The chain of souls around Yu IlHan's body danced around everywhere. Hundreds of chains were being swung around at supersonic speeds thanks to the effect of God Force. They bound, freed, slashed, and pulled bones from higher existences as they swept across the battlefield.

Did Yu IlHan become weaker due to giving up his Deathgod skill? No way. Falling Down was the evolved version of Deathgod. In his domain, every time his enemies died, the power of his flames and souls would amplify! Even if it was someone else who killed the enemy and not Yu IlHan himself!

"Haaaaaaaap!"

Souls and flames spread everywhere, enveloping him. Yu IlHan moved around busily within it. He bound the enemies who tried to flee the battlefield, blocked their attacks, and created crucial opportunities for allies.

The area of Falling Down, where he could initially fight only 10 enemies, grew in power, and now he could fight 20, 30, or even 50.

"Mir, one there!"

[Got it! Kroaaaaaar!]

[BEEAM here! BEEEAM there!]

The ones he could not stop were stopped by Liera and Yumir, and the ones even they could not stop were stopped by the Flying fortress and the Guardian fortress's bombardments! The rays Hundred Eyes shot were fatal to the weakened members of the Destruction Demon Army.

[Aaah.]

[Aaaaaaah, at this rate...]

From one moment, the atmosphere of the battlefield changed once more. It was beyond any rage or resentment, and was in the realm of despair. Perhaps if there was hell, where they had to cry out loud even knowing they could not escape, it was this place.

If there was any salvation for them, it was that, unlike hell, death was allowed to them.

[We cannot reach him with our power.]

[We need to tell them this fact. Perhaps in this place, the fifth.... God may rise.]

"No, you don't."

How dare these guys try to procure a survival route by saying something that sounds cool! Yu IlHan's chains were crueller than the ones like that. If they had explained from the beginning, there would have been no need for foreshadowing! What Yu IlHan and his army needed right now was the power to endure, in other words, experience and their remains for materials!

[Kroaaaaar!]

[The blood.... of the living, the one I lost...!]

At this point in time, the one with the most achievements other than Yu IlHan and Liera was, of course, Paté!

The higher-existence undeads he ruled had now increased to five, and after his blackish-purple mana enveloped the revived higher existences, the properties of their mana changed to harbour a deadly curse. A curse created from a higher existence's mana was, naturally, very effective against higher existences as well.

"Go, eat their flesh and become stronger!"

"No, their flesh must become weapon materials."

"Yes, I'm sorry!"

[Kraaaaaaaak!]

Within the domain of Falling Down, all of Yu IlHan's enemies will be weakened by a league, and they are instead being pushed back by Paté's undead in every way. His arrows chose an enemy, and his undead rushed in to bite and slash at them. A new nightmare descended upon the battlefield.

"We can't lose to Paté. We won't lose to him in our love for His Majesty!"

"You should say loyalty and not love!"

[Show them the power of us wolfkin! Master has himself grabbed the necks of our enemies; if we do not bite them, then it is the embarrassment of us wolves! Will you give the elves all the merits?!]

Even though this was a battlefield where their lives were on the line, the elves and the wolfkin did not back down and charged at the enemies. Who was more suitable for Yu IlHan's special treatment? This was a good opportunity to settle on that. Although this was a very violent and militaristic thought process, it was also a very good one on this battlefield right now.

"Waaaaaaaaaaaaak!"

"I won't die! I'll kill! them"

"Hero is fighting in front of us! The hero is!"

"My gaawwwwd, YuNa's head is breaking! Lady Leytnaaaaa!"

Everyone fought desperately. Each time a higher existence died, people throughout the battlefield became stronger, and one after another acquired 4th classes like Paté. Just fighting against higher existences was an enormous achievement in and of itself after all!

"But why didn't that work for me...."

"Because IlHan is special!"

"Away with you!"

[Khaaaaaak!]

Although Yu IlHan did not directly kill any enemies, simply enveloping the region with Falling Down and swinging his soulflame chains raised his contribution more than anyone else's.

Perhaps because of that, just as he saw he was running out of mana to sustain Falling Down…

[You have become level 295.]

His level rose once again, thanks to the experience he had gained while protecting everyone and restraining the enemies. There were only 5 steps left until level 300, the realm of higher existences!

Of course, it would become even harder from now on, but he was quite surprised that the realm of higher existences, which had felt so distant, was now within sight.

"....Good, I'll think about it after I clean this place up."

He put away the hammer and took out his Eight-tailed dragon spear again, synchronizing with Orochi to enlarge the spear's blade before wrapping it in soulflame chains, making the flames hotter and more translucent. A gigantic spear, over 30 meters long, glowed with a sacred light, the power of the goddess of fire.

"Orochi, please."

[Heh, we're finally rampaging at the last moment.]

Yu IlHan smashed the higher existences that numbered less than twenty now, and prevented them from making a final counterattack. Since he had recovered his mana through a level-up, he didn't even need to save up.

[Kahk!]

[H, him...!]

The higher existences that were wounded and weakened inside the Falling Down domain could not defend against Yu IlHan's spear and had to roll on the ground. Then they were put into a miserable state by the elves and wolfkin who had been aiming for an opportunity all this time.

[Frustrating, and resentful....!]

[Even though I've reached until here....!]

[Kuooooooooh!]

Yumir's roars filled the air. After a hundred rays of light from the Flying Fortress swept the battlefield once, Yumir channelled his wind into the path the beams had passed and dealt additional damage!

[Is that dragon really a lower existence?]

[Ku....gh.]

[Will we, really be..... khaaaak!]

Although Yu IlHan's victory was already set in stone, thanks to the actions of a few members including Paté, the situation cleared up more quickly and surely. Eventually, no enemies were standing on this battlefield!

"It's over."

"The remaining enemies..... none."

"…Won. We won!"

[Kuoooooooh!]

"We have taken victory! We've won against higher existences!"

While Yu IlHan's subordinates cheered, he made the two fortresses float in the air again and took in the remains of higher existences and allies to clean up the battlefield. He subconsciously sighed.

"Fuu."

"IlHan, are you not hurt anywhere?"

"That's what I need to ask. Come here."

"Okay."

Liera asked while approaching Yu IlHan in a completely intact state without a drop of blood on her body, even though she had fought at the front lines. He kissed Liera's forehead and took a step back before making a bitter smile.

"There's no way I'm getting hurt against 5th classes. Although it was slightly dangerous midway..."

As he had activated the option on the gauntlet immediately after seeing that experience points didn't come in, he could safely bypass the danger. The option on the gauntlet was made of the remaining half of Chaos Will, that is.

"I thought it was a technique that only ninjas would use, but it's good that it was of help."

"Yes....The problem is not me but the others."

In this battle, the combined forces of the Dragon's Army, elves, and wolfkin suffered just over 200 deaths.

Considering that the majority of them were below 4th class, it was a miracle. Moreover, the survivors had levelled up by at least five stages, if not more, so it could be called their complete victory.

However, even though it was a complete victory, the dead allies would not come back to their side. They cannot be returned.

"Everyone, listen."

Yu IlHan lined up the army, drunk on the sweet sensation of victory, and declared in a firm voice.

"73 from Mir's Dragon's Army, 59 elves, 75 wolfkin. That's the number of people that died in this fight. As I said before, we will postpone the mourning until later. However, this cannot happen again in the upcoming fights. Remember that your allies will be grieved by your deaths after the battle. Do not die. Understand?"

"Understood!"

"....Everyone fought hard, so rest a little. Paté. You manage your undead from now on."

"Yes!"

The elves and wolfkin returned to the Guardian fortress, while the Dragon's Army returned to the Flying fortress, resolving themselves for the fights to come in their own ways. Mystic, having received orders from Yu IlHan, drove the two fortresses.

The group, having completely wiped out the Destruction Demon Army's frontier camp, left as if nothing had happened. And no one returned there again until the very end of this war.
