=================

The souls making up the chains were linked to Yu IlHan, and so as long as they didn't sever the connection with Yu IlHan's soul, they could not break the chains either.

Of course, those far above Yu IlHan's current league would simply ignore such limitations and escape, but the two in front of him didn't meet this criterion.

"Liera, you take one of them."

"No, let's give it to Mir. Mir is still low level."

Two lower existences were chatting with the bound higher existences in front of them. It was quite ironic, since the scene was like a married couple was trying to give each other the last slice of the meat dish, but ended up giving it to the son.

The demon beast gathered destructive mana in its two hands and puffed up its muscles, while the humanoid formed orbs of ice around him. Each and every one of them had the potential to be fatal! It didn't even take 1 second to do that, as expected of higher existences!

The two attacked at the same time. However, it did not depict Liera being turned into a nice statue. The moment they tried to gather their mana, Yu IlHan called the five shields of Aegis and received all their attacks.

Aegis, now able to absorb and amplify mana before returning it after being upgraded by the feathers of fallen angels, did its job and reflected their mana back at them.

[Kugh!?]

"Good, splendid performance as expected."

And it would become an even more perfect shield when he later enhanced it with materials from higher existences. Yu IlHan was satisfied with Aegis' performance, and unfolded his Ruin Calling to shoot shockwaves in the air to leap.

Now that he had mastered leaping and the Warp skill was advancing by the day, Yu IlHan was at the point where he could stack three or four leaps in a single motion. Even when it looked like he was simply kicking off the air, he would be hundreds of meters away the next moment! It was not that much different from teleportation.

"Hhp."

Following Yu IlHan's leap trajectory, Aegis followed, while the flames that comprised the domain gathered on the tip of his spear. The one Yu IlHan was aiming for was the ice-wielding man who had a higher level. He was planning to break his ice head on.

[Heh, so you find me easy? Did you think you could melt me with your fire?]

This guy was worried that Yu IlHan would just shoot beams from afar, but when he approached, he laughed inwardly, relieved.

[Go on then!]

"Hooh, sure."

This guy thought the three higher existences died just now because they were attacked unexpectedly. Didn't they die in a blink of an eye without being able to do anything? But now, he was dealing with a full frontal assault!

The ones that sought death in arrogance, while not knowing their place! None of them reached the realm of higher existences. He would also become like that!

[This is the ice of Katlar, the hero of the icy plains. Although it's not my preference to freeze a guy... I'll hang out with your delusions for a bit!]

This was the first reason Yu IlHan had judged it fine to go against a higher existence: his flames!

As the higher existence he was facing thought it might be a little dangerous, the other higher existence gave supporting fire, but it was blocked by Aegis, since Yu IlHan already knew they were coming.

"I don't care where you ripped off those lines..."

At that moment, some crunching sounds were heard. Yu IlHan had activated God Force to strengthen his arm muscles, and ended up putting too much pressure on the Eight-tailed dragon spear. This was the second reason Yu IlHan was going up against a higher existence head-on: his muscular strength.

"If you want to read them, buy the official copy!"

[Kugh!?]

Yu IlHan instantly reached the guy's nose with several leaps and struck down with his spear using both hands.

[Critical Hit!]

The guy tried to gather all the frost energy from its body to make several hundred ice swords to attack Yu IlHan, but they all melted in front of the translucent flames from Yu IlHan. Losing in a battle of pure attributes, the guy who had self-proclaimed himself the hero of the icy plains abruptly widened his eyes.

[You broke my ice.... Kugh!?]

[Critical Hit!]

[Critical Hit!]

[Critical....]

A bigger problem was that the spear strikes that contained enough power to force him to use all his ice to block numbered nine more. That was a soundless, formless strike of flames. He could not endure that strike when his league was lowered in the domain of Falling Down.

[Kahk!?]

"You saw it last time, didn't you?"

[Don't tell me that's a martial art even amongst advanced techniques...!? Can't be dodged just by looking...…]

Those were his final words. Yu IlHan's continuous strikes severed all his mana circuits, and the berserk mana froze him whole. He realized that he was the one who had been arrogant at the final moment, and his brain and eyes froze after that as well.

[You have earned experience.]

[You have become level 275. 2 Strength, 1 Agility, 1 Health, 1 Magic increase.]

[You have earned the Lv319 Katlar record.]

The third reason Yu IlHan did not back off against a higher existence was that his Spear techniques had reached a very high level, even among advanced techniques, due to his suffering and years of training.

Even if the prior two conditions weren't met, his Spear of Untraceable Trajectory had enough power to reverse the battle.

[Yes, we indeed cannot win against you.]

The beast, seeing that its stronger ally was being toyed with, gave up trying to survive. At that point, Liera was coming back with Yumir. It realized that Yumir was a dragon, and laughed.

[I knew you were not ordinary, but to think you'd have a dragon. You would be a dragon rider, then.]

Yu IlHan ignored its words and turned his head to Yumir.

"Mir, the experience would decrease since Dad is holding onto it, but you can finish it off."

"Uu, I want to become stronger with my abilities….."

"But you're still weak, so you need to become stronger faster, right?"

"Yeah!"

Yumir made the Breath of Gold long and sharp. His mana was concentrated on that spot, and it became a strong drill as it rotated.

However, just as Yu IlHan was thinking that it would be good to learn wind magic as well, the enemy spoke again; it seemed it hadn't finished last time.

[You must be feeling very confident right now. You must be drunk on the fact that you can overwhelm higher existences. However, behind me are countless 5th-class allies and even stronger 6th-class beings. The war for the higher world has only just started. Heaven's Army and the Army of Brilliant Light – those arrogant bastards will also not welcome an irregular like you ...Can you, a mere lower existence, handle all of them?]

"This guy's nucleus is just under the right waist, so strike there, Mir."

It's very ordinary; the last scream made Yu IlHan feel good. It looked like his contribution was higher, as over 50% of the experience came to him, but Yumir, who finished it off, also rejoiced as he levelled up several times.

"IlHan, I don't think that guy was wrong.... Will you really be alright? I've seen several hundred 5th-class beings clashing for a higher world."

"I'm pretty assured after the battle just now. We'll be fine against five 6th-class beings in the domain of Falling Down. It not only increases my power but also weakens enemies. With you, me, Mir, and the Flying Fortress, we can take on five at once."

Liera had the urge to hit Yu IlHan, but seeing him smile, she couldn't bring herself to do it. He was probably thinking of some absurd things right now. And those absurd thoughts were the main driving force that made the current Yu IlHan.

Perhaps the greatest power Yu IlHan possessed was neither his power, nor his effort, nor his talent, but the sudden insights he had drawn from the knowledge he had accumulated over numerous years – she thought.

"There we go."

Yu IlHan repaired his equipment, which had been damaged in the battle against the higher existences just now, by slamming some magic stones against it, then embraced Yumir in his arms before asking.

"Mir, it will be quite an intense battle from now on. Will it be alright?"

"Yeah, I won't be able to truly become strong if I don't experience suffering. I want to become stronger using fair methods and not sneaky ones like just now."

"Good, it's fine if you're thinking like that."

"And I too."

Yumir seemed to hesitate for a moment, but eventually spoke in frustration, biting his lip.

"Want to avenge Phiria."

"...Yes."

Yumir did spend 2 years and 9 months with Phiria and the others. In fact, he spent more time with them than with Yu IlHan. It wasn't as if Yu IlHan didn't know that, but hearing it from his own mouth was a different feeling altogether.

"...Really, this is not a conversation that should happen between a father and son. Though, I don't disagree with you."
