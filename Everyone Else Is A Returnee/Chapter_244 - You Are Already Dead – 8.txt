Echjar instantly realized what had happened to him. He noticed that an unfamiliar mana had wrapped around him the moment Warp activated. However, at that moment, he was controlling his magic and could not push back or evade that mana. Like that, he was swept into the dimensional transportation. With no time to cancel his magic, it attacked Yu IlHan and Liera, and, naturally, his membership in the Destruction Demon Army was revoked.

[Kuaaaaaaaak!]

[Just, what...]

"So this is the cowardly method you mentioned? How can it be so cowardly...?"

"Didn't I say exactly that it would be very cowardly?"

In fact, this was also the highest achievement his Record skill had gotten so far. The moment he was careless, the moment he could only be swept away by the skill even while knowing that it was happening, and the moment direct harm was inflicted on Yu IlHan and Liera!

All the records he had gathered using the Record skill were used to spot that one moment. And the result was a big success. That guy was spewing blood and leaking mana everywhere!

[Kill, I'll kill kill you! I WILL KILL YOU!]

Of course, as the realm he had reached was very high, it wasn't as if he fell to a lower existence right away, but despite that, the steadily dropping league and the steadily leaking mana were inflicting horrible wounds and excruciating pain.

However, if there was something that frustrated him, it was that Yu IlHan and Liera were only taking damage from his magic and not acting directly, so the concealment hadn't worn off!

[Come out! I SAID COME OUT!]

[Would you have come out if you heard that? Truly laughable, no, truly frightening....]

While he emitted mana in all directions as he roared in pain, Spiera, who had slightly recovered, re-gripped the pure-white spear and laughed. Spiera had also been attacked by the dragon after coming to Earth, so her qualifications would not get stripped even if she attacked!

[Truly an interesting man.]

Thinking of such a thing and putting it into action as well, who could have imagined that? She was no longer afraid of the enemy. Although she was the same in that regard, her body was exhausted; she was not alone here.

[Well then. The one in front of you is me, dragon. I don't think you can escape death at this rate... but wouldn't you love to rampage to your heart's content before that?]

[You dare laugh at me?]

Echjar roared. A 7th class was a 7th class after all. The magic that flowed from his control momentarily reversed into his mouth, and the next moment it changed into black flames and shot out like a ray of light!

[Kugh!?]

"Kyak!"

"Insane...!?"

The large-scale beam of destruction, which made not only Spiera, who was a little relieved right now, but also Yu IlHan and Liera, who were still concealed, shiver in fear, destroyed everything in its path and made a gigantic hole in the ground.

That was not the end, and the mana harbouring a frightening curse instantly polluted the surrounding region and encroached on other areas! If Earth had a will, it would have expelled him long ago!

[Kraoooooooooooooooh!]

A roar, dense with mana, unbelievable for one who was losing his league, filled the air.

[I am not strong because I reached the 7th class. I! COULD REACH 7TH CLASS BECAUSE I WAS STRONG!]

[Hmph.]

Spiera was the same in that regard: she was proud to have reached the realm she was in. She spread her two pairs of wings and evaded the dragon's attacks by using all the mana in her surroundings. The mana crystals on the surface of her spear peeled off one by one and started to rotate around her.

[Trying to harm me using mana? FOOLISH!]

[We won't know if we don't try. No, seeing as you can't even control your own mana, I think the outcome is obvious!]

A 7th class losing his league clashed against a gravely wounded 6th class. However, Echjar was still a lot stronger than Spiera. Like the time he shouted out a beam of destruction, he did not just let go of the mana leaking from his body, and used it to attack Spiera instead.

[Khaaaaaaaaaah![

[Kufufu….. *cough*, you look horrible for someone who shouted so arrogantly, angel!]

[Hah, you too....!]

The white blood bursting from Spiera's body formed a rainbow in the air. Of course, the dragon was not in a good state either. Not because he had been injured by Spiera, but because the more it tried to move its body and control the mana, the faster his body was destroyed.

"Uaaaah, just looking at someone falling to a lower existence is horrible. I remember back then."

"I'm fine with you sticking to me, but please, save it for later."

Meanwhile, Yu IlHan was estimating when he would attack that guy. Although he did not expect the enemy to be weakened so quickly, he never imagined that guy would last so long either! However, thanks to that, another one of his hopes became achievable.

"Although I had given up by coming to Earth, perhaps I might be able to get intact 7th class remains."

"My IlHan, this noona likes your unchanging attitude to things."

And so, Yu IlHan was aiming for only one thing – the moment he was weakened enough that he could no longer block Yu IlHan's attack, while the body still retained its value as a 7th class!

'That's mine. I can't give that to Spiera, or the Heaven's Army.'

In fact, Spiera didn't wound that guy much either, so if Yu IlHan's attack succeeded, he would be able to take the remains fair and square. There was only one thing that tugged at his mind – the possibility that the dragon was preparing something in response to Yu IlHan's surprise attack.

"But even so, I need to get in there before it's too late."

The spear that flew out with a push of his hand killed about three monsters on the ground, and the Deathgod skill activated, enhancing Yu IlHan's abilities.

After that, he threw the spear around two more times to achieve a 100% increase in all his abilities, and checked that Na YuNa's blessing was still in effect and imbued the spear with mana. Meanwhile, Liera drew her spear and took a deep breath to calm herself.

"I'll take it after you."

"He might have put up self-defence measures. Though I'm not sure if it will be effective now that he has fallen to a lower existence... But rather than trying to inflict damage, please deflect any possible counterattacks."

"Got it."

Even right now, Spiera was having a hard time against the enemy. He wouldn't have brought her here if he wanted her to die. Yu IlHan did not hesitate anymore and charged forward.

Blaze, purple flame, Eternal Flame, and Dragonic blood! The strongest flame Yu IlHan could make without Falling Down burned in a translucent ball at the tip of his spear.

'Fuuuuu.'

The moment he struck out, the Great Cosmos-severing Spear that Spiera had shown him replayed in his mind!

'Of course, there's no way I could do that if it was possible just by seeing her demonstration once.'

Although it was quite frustrating to acknowledge, by this time he wondered if the problem was with his talent. Skills weren't so easily learned just because someone taught you or because you were qualified to learn them.

However, if he tried harder, he could reach that realm someday. The path Yu IlHan had walked until now was like that after all. Yu IlHan hid his short thoughts and picked up his spear. Now Echjar's gigantic body was right before his eyes.

'The sharpness of the sword, the speed of the whip, the strength of a blunt weapon. And physical combat that ties all of them into the spear!'

And using all of that, while striking several times with the Spear of Untraceable...!

"Hhhhhhhhhp!"

[Got you!]

'Kugh!?"

However, the moment Yu IlHan's spear drove a deep wound into the gigantic body, the dragon caught Yu IlHan's position with frightening reflexes and immediately focused his mana on the wounded spot.

[Yu IlHan!?]

"Hbbb!"

A Jetstream of blood burst out from the wound without any delay! From the frightening, deadly aura within that blood, the identity was a vicious curse that Yu IlHan had experienced before. As it used blood as the medium, it required sacrifice from the user and was just as vicious and unrelenting a curse!

Though he didn't know it was an ability that reacted to the moment of an attack, he did indeed expect a counterattack. Yu IlHan immediately tried to escape the place and pass the baton to Liera and Spiera, but...

"Ugh!? Uhhhhhp!"

"Eeeek, this is!"

It didn't go as he wished– he could not move, and Liera's spear could not cut apart the blood.

[Frightening resistance alright. If any more time had passed, I might have failed....!] The gigantic dragon muttered as it poured black blood from every part of its body. All the leaking blood flowed in accordance with the curse the dragon had used as a final attack, assaulting and restricting Yu Ilhan.

[Even though I'm in the midst of my fall, to think you can resist the curse I've used my blood and mana to inflict... are you really a lower existence?]

"Wha, jus, wha….." Yu IlHan muttered in shock, but the blood already covered his entire body, and he couldn't even speak properly. As if to drown Yu IlHan to death in his blood, the dragon was pouring out endless streams of blood!

"Eeeeeeeih! The blood does not cut so well! IlHaaaaan!"

[Li, LIera! Stop. That curse is spiritually connecting Yu IlHan and Echjar directly. If you attack Echjar right now, you may inflict damage on Yu IlHan as well!]

Liera tried to cancel the curse with her spear, but as Spiera said, the dragon's curse had a complex spiritual structure, and if something went wrong, even Yu IlHan would be put in dire straits.

Although he was prepared for any counterattack of power, Yu IlHan never imagined that what the dragon had prepared was a curse that even his higher curse resistance could not block. He was gritting his teeth in the pitch-black world of blood.

Echjar laughed heartily even as his spirit and body were being weakened in real time upon seeing Yu IlHan's situation.

[My main source of intake is despair and fear. I become stronger as you despair!]

"Even though you're being weakened....!"

However, thinking back, Liera also possessed her angel powers for quite a long time while she fought against Chaos Will. It was not surprising that Echjar, a 7th class, was holding out longer.

Thinking of the curse's power that seeped into Yu IlHan's body, boiling his blood and burning his flesh, Yu IlHan's death would come faster than the dragon's fall.

'Fu, fuuu....'

The Infernal Dragon's body had no way to counter curses, so it couldn't do anything to the curse either – he had added another option instead of something related to curses, since he believed in his own curse resistance. He thought he was a fool for being completely relieved of curses, since there was no higher stage of curse resistance.

So by 7th class, one can ignore even a mastered skill! Even if it was one during a fall to a lower realm! Yu IlHan acquired a new realization. Though, it was slightly late.

"IlHan!"

[Dammit, I did hear that you were one of the most vicious and evil of the dragons, but...!]

[Blabber all you want. Yes, if I kill this human and absorb his records, then I might be able to return to Dareu! Then, only you will remain in this closed world... huh?]

Having felt that he could not resist with just curse resistance, Yu IlHan mobilized all the powers in his body to resist.

First was Falling Down, something that could be seen as a higher version of curse resistance! In his Falling Down domain, his recovery rate will also increase by 5x. If he brought up his Transcendent regeneration to the limit to resist, then perhaps!

[You really are no ordinary human... I will have to kill you off here!]

The pressure from the curse became stronger. The blood that seeped into every single hole in his body was frightening!

Yu IlHan tried to focus all his flames within the Falling Down domain to burn the blood, but the curse did not disappear even after the blood had become vapour; instead, it clung to him, making him even more agonized. Flames were no good!

'Then...!'

Right now, the dragon definitely said that the curse would not dissolve unless one of the two, Yu IlHan or himself, dies. That meant that if Yu IlHan somehow survived this curse, the one to die would become the dragon instead!

"Even though you're falling to a lower existence without being able to do anything, you want to kill me....? Let's do it then!"

Yu IlHan gritted his teeth and activated Dragonic blood. Of course, the target of enhancement was Transcendent regeneration! In the domain of Falling Down, if he could not defeat the curse, even with Transcendent regeneration enhanced by Dragonic blood, he would welcome death!

However, at the moment he activated Dragonic blood, something completely unexpected reacted inside of him.

It was the aura of a dragon that seeped into him from the moment he got into a contract with Yumir.
